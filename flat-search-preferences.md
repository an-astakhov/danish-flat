# Flat search preferences

I am looking for an entire rental apartment in Copenhagen, not a room or shared-housing offer. Focus on 3- or 4-room homes, preferably at least 85 m². Base rent should ideally be no more than DKK 23,000 per month; up to DKK 26,000 is acceptable only for an especially strong match. Utilities may be additional.

Prioritize Copenhagen S, Copenhagen SV, Islands Brygge, Havneholmen, Teglholmen, Sluseholmen, Nordhavn, and nearby well-connected areas. A practical public-transport journey to both Nordhavn and Havneholmen matters. Prefer apartments not on 1st or 2nd floor, and very big bonus for being the last floor; however, do not dismiss 1st and 2nd floors if a good match. Balcony, private terrace, private garden are a plus, but not a must. 

The apartment should be available no later than November 1, 2026. Prefer unlimited, long-term, or at least 1 year rentals. Exclude listings that are clearly reserved or unavailable. Rank down ads with unusually high move-in costs, unclear rental periods, no CPR registration, but retain them when they otherwise fit and make the caveat visible.

Rank up slightly ads from large corporate landlords, such as EDC, since this is a safer option. But do not avoid private listings altogether.

Add up to five strong new matches per search run. Unknown optional details are acceptable; unknown hard requirements are not.
