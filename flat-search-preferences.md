# Flat search preferences

This is a provisional brief inferred from the flats currently in the tracker. Edit it freely before invoking `$search-flats`; ordinary prose and bullets are both fine.

I am looking for an entire rental apartment in Copenhagen, not a room or shared-housing offer. Focus on 3- or 4-room homes, preferably at least 90 m². Base rent should ideally be no more than DKK 23,000 per month; up to DKK 26,000 is acceptable only for an especially strong match. Utilities may be additional.

Prioritize Copenhagen S, Copenhagen SV, Islands Brygge, Havneholmen, Teglholmen, Sluseholmen, Ørestad, Nordhavn, and nearby well-connected areas. A practical public-transport journey to both Nordhavn and Havneholmen matters. Prefer a balcony, private terrace, or garden, but outdoor space is not mandatory.

The apartment should be available no later than November 1, 2026. Prefer unlimited or genuinely long-term rentals. Exclude listings that are clearly reserved or unavailable. Rank down ads with unusually high move-in costs, unclear rental periods, no CPR registration, or photos that do not show the actual unit, but retain them when they otherwise fit and make the caveat visible.

Add up to five strong new matches per search run. Unknown optional details are acceptable; unknown hard requirements are not.
